Our Origin

Born from a Vision
of Excellence

LuxeEstate was founded in 2009 by Alexander Mercer, a former investment banker who saw a critical gap in the market: ultra-high-net-worth clients deserved a property firm that matched their standards of excellence in every interaction.

From a single office on Park Avenue with a team of five, we set out to redefine what luxury real estate meant — not just in terms of properties, but in the calibre of relationships, the depth of market intelligence, and the impeccable quality of service.

Today, we operate in 42 countries with over 340 dedicated advisors. Yet our founding principle remains unchanged: every client deserves to feel they are our only client.

Alexander Mercer Founder & Chairman, LuxeEstate
